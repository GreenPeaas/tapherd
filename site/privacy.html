Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-19

The short version

The free TapHerd connector runs on your computer and collects nothing. This website stores your email only if you join the Pro waitlist. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and counts page views without tracking you. Demo videos are served from this site; nothing loads from video services.

What we collect

WhatWhenWhere it's kept
Your email address and the date you signed upOnly if you join the Pro waitlistA private table in our Supabase project. Visitors can add an address but can't read the list.
Anonymous page-view counts (pages, referrers, country, browser type)When you visit this site (Cloudflare Web Analytics is on)Cloudflare Web Analytics. No cookies, no fingerprinting, and no tracking across sites.

What we don't collect

  • The free connector has no telemetry. Your screenshots, device data, and optional action logs stay on your computer.
  • TapHerd never asks for, stores, or sees your social media passwords. You sign in to apps on your own device.
  • The website has no advertising trackers and sets no cookies.

How we use your email

We use it only to email you about TapHerd Pro: early access, the founder offer, and an occasional product update. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We don't sell or share your email.

Delete your email

Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or write to support@tapherd.info from that address and ask us to delete it. We'll remove it from the waitlist within 30 days and confirm by email.

Questions

Email support@tapherd.info. For security issues, see how to report a vulnerability.
