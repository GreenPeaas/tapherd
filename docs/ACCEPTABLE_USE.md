# Acceptable use

Use TapHerd to post content to accounts you own. TapHerd's recipes and scheduler only support posting. Generic controls are subject to the acceptable use policy. All planned paid features support posting workflows only.

These activities are prohibited.

- Creating, registering, or verifying accounts.
- Automating likes, follows, comments, direct messages, views, or any other engagement.
- Scraping other users' data.
- Solving CAPTCHAs.
- Spoofing device fingerprints, evading emulator detection, rotating proxies, or attempting to conceal automation.
